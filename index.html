THERMO-SHIELD: Solar Defense Habitat

InnovateX '26

My team's first hackathon. We co-designed a portable, zero-fuel thermal shelter concept for DRDO problem statement 26051, aimed at soldiers at high-altitude posts such as Ladakh. It captures solar energy by day, stores the heat in a Phase Change Material (PCM) layer, and releases it at night, with aerogel and PUF insulation keeping it in.

  • Design target: 16–20 °C inside at −30 °C outside
  • Sleeps 2–4 people per module
  • Packs into a 120 × 60 × 40 cm bag, about 45–60 kg
  • Set up in under 15 minutes

A concept design with target specifications. The poster images are concept renders, not photos of a built prototype.
